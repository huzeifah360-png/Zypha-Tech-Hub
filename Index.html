About Us

Practical technology education and ICT services, based in Abuja.

What Zypha Tech Hub Is

Zypha Tech Hub is a technology training and ICT services company based in Dei-Dei, Bwari, Abuja. We bring together structured computer training programmes, everyday ICT services — cyber café, printing, scanning and documentation support — and digital solutions such as data analysis and web development, in one accessible hub.

Our purpose is straightforward: to make practical digital skills and reliable ICT support available to students, individuals and small businesses, and to support learners and clients well beyond a single transaction or class.

Mission

To deliver practical, hands-on technology education and dependable ICT services that help learners and clients build real, usable digital skills.

Vision

To grow into a trusted technology hub across Abuja and beyond — where innovation and learning consistently meet, for every learner who walks in.

Core Values

Practicality, honesty in what we offer, accessibility of digital skills, and steady support for the people we train and serve.

What We Do

From first computer lessons to data and web skills.

Technology education

Certificate and Diploma programmes covering computer appreciation through to data and business intelligence tools.

Practical digital skills

Word processing, spreadsheets, design and internet skills taught for everyday and workplace use.

ICT services

Cyber café, printing, scanning, laminating and documentation support for students and the public.

Digital empowerment

Helping individuals and small businesses use technology — from registrations to data and web presence.

Meet the Founder

Huzeifah Abdulazeez

B.Sc. Computer Science and Data Science

Zypha Tech Hub was established from a vision of bringing technology education, practical digital skills and ICT services together in one place. The name "Zypha" is derived from the founder's own name, Huzeifah — it represents the identity behind the hub.

With a background in Computer Science and Data Science, the founder's focus has stayed on technology, education, data and web development — and on making those areas genuinely reachable for learners who are starting out, as well as individuals and businesses that need practical ICT support.

“My focus has always been technology, education and data — and helping people turn that into skills they can actually use.”

— Huzeifah Abdulazeez, Founder, Zypha Tech Hub

That combination — innovation and learning — is where the company motto comes from, and it continues to guide how Zypha Tech Hub's programmes and services are built.
